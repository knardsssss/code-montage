# Example video brief

- Project ID: 3points-newsletter-test-001
- Title: 3Points Compliance — Financial Compliance News Update
- Duration: 45–60 seconds
- Voiceover language: English
- Subtitles language: Traditional Chinese
- Newspaper path: resources/documents/SFC Reprimands Luk Fook Securities.pdf
- Remarks: 
### Objective
Create a draft compliance news video explaining the supplied news
item and its practical lessons for financial-services firms.
End by encouraging viewers to follow 3Points Compliance.

### Audience
Financial-services professionals and compliance teams.

### Source material
News PDF:
resources/documents/SFC-Reprimands-Luk-Fook-Securities.pdf

Official supplied logo:
resources/media/3Points-logo.jpg

This is a manually extracted news item for a prototype test,
not the full original newsletter.

### Factual controls
Read the PDF before writing the script.
Use the PDF as the factual source for this test.

Preserve the company’s full name and distinguish the regulator,
the firm, the cybersecurity deficiencies, and the practical lessons.

The supplied PDF reports a "$2.1 million" fine.
Do not silently assume a currency that the PDF does not specify.
Flag the currency for verification before external publication.

Do not invent incident dates, regulatory announcement dates,
client losses, stolen data, or details about the ransomware attack.

Do not imply the supplied summary is an independently verified
copy of the regulator’s original announcement.
Record the supplied PDF filename and page number in the
supporting source notes.

Distinguish reported findings from general compliance lessons.

### Suggested structure
Opening: a concise factual hook about cybersecurity and compliance.
Main section: explain the reported action and key control failures.
Takeaway: summarise practical controls firms should review.
Final 5–7 seconds: branded closing screen.

The closing screen is included within the total duration.

### Branding
Use the supplied logo as an image.
Preserve its proportions, colours, wording, and complete design.
Do not recreate the logo with AI or crop out the company name.

Use a white background for the closing screen.
Keep the logo clearly visible and separate from subtitles.
Do not invent brand colours, website addresses, or social handles.

### Closing call to action
Closing voiceover:
“Follow 3Points Compliance for regular updates on legal news
in finance.”

On-screen closing text:
“Follow 3Points Compliance”
“Legal and regulatory updates for financial services”

Do not claim that 3Points Compliance investigated the case,
represents the SFC, or guarantees regulatory compliance.

### Visual approach
Use simple typography, icons, and restrained motion graphics.
Avoid paid stock footage and generated photorealistic scenes.
Do not use invented screenshots or fabricated regulatory documents.
Do not use dramatic visuals suggesting facts absent from the source.

### Format
First-test proposal: vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920.
Use readable Traditional Chinese subtitles that do not overlap the branding.

### Review workflow
Check that both input files can be opened.
Prepare a proposed script and storyboard for approval.
Do not render until I approve this first test.
Do not publish automatically.

### Budget
Use only models explicitly marked free, opensource and approved local assets.
Do not purchase assets, use paid APIs, or add subscriptions.
Ask before downloading extra models or installing software.
Report missing narration or rendering dependencies rather than
silently switching to a paid service.
